COMMON INQUIRIES

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What benefits do sponsors receive when partnering with our robotics teams?

Sponsors gain premier brand visibility on our robot chassis, team apparel, event banners, international tournament livestreams, and within our official Engineering Portfolio distributed to judges and industry leaders worldwide.

What is FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) and why does it matter?

FIRST Tech Challenge is the world's premier high school robotics competition founded by inventor Dean Kamen. Students design, build, and program robots to compete in dynamic 2v2 arena games with a 30-second autonomous period and a 2-minute driver-controlled period.

How do ARISE 39081 and GreenAms 24751 cooperate?

Both ARISE 39081 and GreenAms 24751 are FIRST Tech Challenge sister teams from Hanoi - Amsterdam High School for Gifted ("When I say Ams, you say Rise!" & "When I say Green, you say Ams!"). We share research facilities, co-develop open-source robotics like Bamboo Bot, host collaborative STEM workshops (GART Expo, Wonderlab), and build a world-class training pipeline for Vietnamese students.

How can high school students join our robotics teams?

We hold annual recruitment trials every autumn for Hanoi - Amsterdam students, welcoming passionate minds across mechanical design, programming, electronics, and business outreach. No prior experience is required—only curiosity and dedication!

How can other teams or organizations collaborate with us?

Teams and organizations can partner with us through technical mentorship, co-hosted workshops, STEM camps, sponsorship, or open-source CAD & code exchanges. We warmly welcome collaborations that empower the robotics community in Vietnam and worldwide.
